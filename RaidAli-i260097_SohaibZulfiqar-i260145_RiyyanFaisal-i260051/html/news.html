Announcements

VEXON has opened registration for the 1–4 January festival at FAST-NUCES Islamabad. Teams may enter the programming contest, hackathon, futsal and esports events. Submit team names and fees before 20 December. Confirmed teams receive their venue timetable by email.

Societies can request the Main Auditorium, CS Lab 3 or Cricket Ground through the Resources page. The events desk checks date, equipment and safety needs. Requests for January programmes should arrive by 1 December.

The FAST-NUCES Islamabad Robotics Club won the National Campus Robotics Challenge in Lahore. Their line-following rover completed the final course after a late sensor repair. VEXON will display the rover near CS Lab 3 during New Year Fest 27.

New proposals must include a venue, date, team size, budget and safety contact. The events department reviews academic and student society proposals every Friday. Use the Event Proposal Form before announcing a campus programme.
