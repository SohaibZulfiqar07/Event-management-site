Event Resources

Use these simple guides before bringing a New Year Fest 27 activity to FAST-NUCES Islamabad.

Book a venue

  1. Choose an available date and venue.
  2. Complete the Venue Booking Form with equipment needs.
  3. Send the request to the events desk at least 14 days early.
  4. Wait for written confirmation before advertising.

Available venues include Main Auditorium, CS Lab 3, Cricket Ground, Student Centre and Seminar Hall.

Event rules

  1. Keep the confirmed schedule and venue capacity visible.
  2. Bring student cards for team check-in.
  3. Keep cables, exits and walkways clear.
  4. Use approved sound levels in the Main Auditorium.
  5. Return borrowed equipment after the programme.
