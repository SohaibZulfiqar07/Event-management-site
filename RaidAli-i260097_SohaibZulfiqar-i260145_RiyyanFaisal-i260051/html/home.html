New Year Fest 27 at FAST-NUCES Islamabad

New Year Fest 27 brings FAST-NUCES Islamabad together from 1 to 4 January for code, music, sport and career events built by students.

Meet competitors in CS Lab 3, performers in the Main Auditorium and teams at the Cricket Ground during VEXON’s campus festival.
